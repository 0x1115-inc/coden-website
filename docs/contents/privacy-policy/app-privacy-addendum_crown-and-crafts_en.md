# **App Privacy Addendum**

**Supplement to the Master Privacy Policy of CODEN COMPANY LIMITED**  
*Last updated: 2026-10-12*  
This Crown & Crafts App Privacy Addendum (“Addendum”) supplements the **Master Privacy Policy of CODEN COMPANY LIMITED** (“Master Policy”) and applies specifically to the **Crown & Crafts game** (“Game”) on iOS, Android and web game portals.  
By playing Crown & Crafts, you agree to this Addendum and the Master Policy. In the event of any inconsistency, this Addendum prevails for matters relating to the Game.

## **1 - About Crown & Crafts**

Crown & Crafts is a medieval logistics strategy game: players build roads, haul goods by hand and cart, and supply a kingdom. The Game is developed and published by CODEN COMPANY LIMITED (“CODEN”).  
CODEN COMPANY LIMITED acts as the data controller for data processed by the Game.  
The Game has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number.  
For general privacy practices, please refer to the Master Privacy Policy available at:  
[https://coden.org/privacy-policy](https://coden.org/privacy-policy)

## **3 - Information collected through the Game**

### **A. Game data stored on your device**

Your progress, settings and the items you own are saved on your device, or on web portals in the portal’s own save system. The Game also creates a random ID for your installation. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the browser’s site data.

### **B. Bug reports you choose to send (web versions)**

Web versions of the Game have a “Report a bug” button. A report is sent only when you press Send. It contains:

- The message you write.
- The game version, your platform (web, Android or iOS), operating system and browser.
- A snapshot of the current game: map, saved state, recent in-game events and frame rate.
- The random installation ID.

Our server also receives your IP address with the request, as any web server does. Reports are used only to find and fix problems. They are stored on our server and forwarded to a private chat channel our team uses to review them. Please do not write personal information in the message.

### **C. Advertising (iOS and Android versions with ads)**

These versions show ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels. Google AdMob may collect:

- Your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you allow tracking).
- Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it.
- Device type, operating system, app version and how you interact with ads.

Google uses this information to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, and, with your consent, to personalize ads. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your choices:

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Privacy settings in the Game’s Settings menu.
- On iOS, the Game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings, under Google › Ads or Privacy › Ads.
- Buying “Remove Ads” removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay available as an option.

### **D. Analytics and crash reports (Android, and iOS versions that include them)**

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played: for example which levels are started and finished, tutorial steps, ads watched and items bought. Firebase collects an app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, country (derived from the IP address) and these game events. Firebase’s advertising features are turned off, so this data is not used for ads. When a version includes Firebase Crashlytics, it sends a crash report (device state and the error) so we can fix the crash.

### **E. In-app purchases**

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). CODEN never sees your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation of which items you own, so the Game can unlock them and restore them on a new device.

### **F. Web game portals**

On portals such as CrazyGames, the portal runs the website, its ads, its user accounts and its saves under its own privacy policy ([CrazyGames Privacy Policy](https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy)). CODEN does not receive your portal account details. Web versions do not use AdMob or Firebase, and send data to CODEN only when you send a bug report.

## **4 - How we use information**

- To run the Game and keep your progress and purchases.
- To show and measure ads in versions with ads.
- To learn which parts of the Game work, and to fix bugs and crashes.
- To prevent fraud and abuse.

CODEN does not sell your personal information.

- Contract: to provide the Game and the items you buy.
- Consent: personalized ads and tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- Legitimate interests: analytics, bug reports you send, non-personalized ads that keep the Game free, and security.

## **8 - User rights**

- **Ads:** change your consent under Privacy settings in the Game’s Settings menu. On iOS, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID.
- **Game data:** uninstall the Game or clear the site data.
- **Bug reports:** to see or delete a report, email legal@coden.org with the game name and the report code shown after you sent it. We reply within 30 days.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority.
- **California:** CODEN does not sell personal information. Personalized ads may count as “sharing” under California law. You can opt out with the ad choices above.

## **9 - Children’s Privacy**

The Game is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your country.  
CODEN does not knowingly collect personal information from children.  
If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## **10 - Security**

Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Only our team can access bug reports.  
While we implement commercially reasonable safeguards, no system can be fully secure.

## **11 - International data transfers**

Google and the other services above may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Bug reports are processed and stored in Vietnam.
