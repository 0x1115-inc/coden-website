## **3 - Information we collect**

CODEN may collect or process personal information in the course of delivering our services or developing digital solutions.

### **A. Information provided directly**
This may include:

- Contact information (email, name, phone number).  
- Project-related information or business documentation.  
- User-submitted content (images, text, uploads).  
- Account or authentication data when required for specific services.  
- Support inquiries or feedback.

  ### **B. Information collected automatically**

Depending on the service or application:

- Device information.  
- Diagnostic logs.  
- Crash reports.  
- System usage data.

CODEN does not engage in analytics or tracking unless expressly stated in service-specific or app-specific notice.

### **C. Information processed on behalf of clients**

During consulting, development, testing, pentesting, or data processing engagements, CODEN may receive or handle client-owned data.

- Such data is processed solely according to the client’s instructions.  
- CODEN maintains confidentiality and adheres to contractual and legal requirements.

CODEN processes personal information for the following purposes:

- Providing support and responding to requests.  
- Fulfilling contractual obligations to clients.

- Conducting pentesting or security assessments when engaged.  
- Complying with legal requirements.

## **5 - Legal bases for processing**

Where required by applicable data protection laws, CODEN processes personal information on the basis of:

- Performance of a contract.  
- Compliance with legal obligations.  
- Legitimate interests.  
- Consent (when explicitly required).  
- Protection of vital interests.  
- Performance of tasks for clients under data processing agreements.
