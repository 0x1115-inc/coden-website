# **App Privacy Addendum**

**Supplement to the Master Privacy Policy of CODEN COMPANY LIMITED**  
*Last updated: 2025-12-02*  
This Green Vector App Privacy Addendum (“Addendum”) supplements the **Master Privacy Policy of CODEN COMPANY LIMITED** (“Master Policy”) and applies specifically to the **Green Vector mobile application** (“App”).  
By using the Green Vector App, you agree to this Addendum and the Master Policy. In the event of any inconsistency, this Addendum prevails for matters relating to the App.

## **1 - About the Green Vector App**

The Green Vector App is a mobile application designed to support users in diagnosing plant health, managing plant care, and organizing plant-related information. The App is developed and published by CODEN COMPANY LIMITED (“CODEN”) and provided for use by customers of Green Vector.  
CODEN COMPANY LIMITED acts as the data controller for all data processed within the App.  
For general privacy practices, please refer to the Master Privacy Policy available at:  
[https://coden.org/privacy-policy](https://coden.org/privacy-policy)

## **2 - Information collected through the App**

The App is designed to store user-provided data locally on the user’s device, with the specific exception of plant images submitted to the “Plant Doctor” feature, which are temporarily transmitted to a third-party AI service for analysis. CODEN does not operate backend servers or cloud databases for this App, and no user account is currently required.

### **A. Information You Provide**
The App collects and processes the following data when you choose to provide it:

- Plant images submitted through the “Plant Doctor” feature.  
- Plan information added to the “My Garden" feature.  
- Care schedule and reminder preferences.  
- Task completions and plant care history.

No sensitive personal information is requested or required.

## **3 - Image processing for Plant Diagnosis**

When you submit a plant image for diagnosis:

- The image is transmitted to OpenAI’s ChatGPT API for AI-based visual analysis.
- Only the plant image is sent. The image is not stored by CODEN and, according to OpenAI’s API data-usage policy, will not be used to train OpenAI models.
- CODEN does not store any copy of the image outside your device.
- No metadata (GPS, EXIF, timestamps) is extracted.
- Images are used exclusively to generate plant-health analysis results.

The OpenAI API processes your data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

## **4 - Local data storage**

All App data, including plant profiles, care schedules, reminder logs, and photos stored for the “My Garden” feature, is kept exclusively on your device. This excludes the temporary transmission of a plant image to the Groq API when using the “Plant Doctor” feature, as detailed in section 2-A.  
CODEN does not:

- Access your photos.  
- Upload your data to any server (except for the transient image analysis as described in Section 2-A).  
- Synchronize data across devices.
- Store user information in cloud databases.

You maintain full control over the data stored in the App.

## **5 - Push notifications**

The App uses device notifications for:

- Care reminders  
- Scheduled plant care tasks

Notifications are generated locally on your device and do not require sending personal data to any external service.  
The App does not send marketing notifications.

## **6 - Third-party services**

The App uses the following external services:

### **A. OpenAI API (ChatGPT – AI Image and Text Analysis)**
The App uses the OpenAI ChatGPT API to analyze images submitted for diagnosis and to support AI-based conversational guidance.
Your plant image and your chat messages may be transmitted to OpenAI as part of the analysis request.
OpenAI does not use API data to train its models, in accordance with its API data-usage policy.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising frameworks, or tracking technologies are integrated into the App.

## **7 - Data Retention**

Because all App data is stored locally on your device:

- Data remains on your device until you remove it.  
- You may delete plant profiles, images, or the entire App at any time.  
- Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data associated with it.

CODEN does not retain any copies of your data.

## **8 - User rights**

Since all data is stored locally, you may exercise your rights directly by managing the information on your device, including:

- Viewing your stored plant information.  
- Deleting images.  
- Removing plant profiles.  
- Modifying care schedules.  
- Uninstalling the App to erase all data.

If you contact CODEN for a data request, we may only address matters within our actual control, as we do not store user data on servers.

## **9 - Children’s Privacy**

The App is not intended for children under 13\.  
CODEN does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.  
If we become aware of such data, we will take immediate actions to delete it.

## **10 - Security**

To protect user privacy:

- The App operates without cloud storage.  
- No user accounts are required.  
- Most processing occurs on the device.  
- Only plant images provided by the user are sent to Groq for analysis.

While we implement commercially reasonable safeguards, no system can be fully secure.

## **11 - International data transfers**

When using the “Plant Doctor” feature or AI chat, your plant images and messages may be transmitted to servers operated by OpenAI, which may be located outside your country of residence.
By using these features, you consent to such data transfers.
